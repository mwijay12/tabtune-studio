The panel

One panel, and it remembers where you were

Recorder, player, lyric editor and karaoke view sit in a single panel. Dock it to the side of the window and it stays open while you work in the tab.

  • Dock it. The pin button moves the studio into the side panel, where a stray click cannot dismiss it mid-take.
  • It remembers. Ten takes live in IndexedDB. Reopen the panel and the newest one reloads itself, with a note saying what came back.
  • It rotates. Paste as many Gemini keys as you have. When one is rate limited the next one picks up the call.
  • It packs. One .zip: the audio, the synced .lrc, the plain words, and a sheet with duration, bitrate and capture time.
